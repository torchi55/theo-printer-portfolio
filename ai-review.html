Selected Projects

Helioform Station
Spring '26  ·  Team project with Marcus Chan & Kairei Jones  ·  Grasshopper / Environmental Systems

A transit and service station in Page, Arizona, designed primarily in Grasshopper with Ladybug environmental analysis. The facade brick layout was not applied as texture — it was derived directly from solar exposure simulation. Ladybug mapped actual solar angles and intensity gradients onto the facade geometry; the brick pattern rationalized out of that data. The project includes CSI-coded 211 Materials construction documentation, which is not standard student work.

Helioform is the strongest project in the portfolio technically. It required running Grasshopper definitions for environmental performance, reading the output, and translating it into a buildable facade system — all simultaneously. That pipeline from simulation to construction documentation is what firms spend years teaching junior staff. Theo did it as a third-year project.

Farm to Brick
Spring '25  ·  Studio 105B  ·  Modular Systems

A multi-level farmers-market pavilion organized on a continuous modular grid, inspired by LEGO: stalls, retail spaces, and green areas each occupy an exactly defined cubic volume designed for reconfiguration over time, stacked above a ground plane of food-truck parking. Four found objects — a LEGO head, torso, legs, and jackhammer — are suspended within the wire framework as programmatic anchors.

The physical model was produced in translucent 3D-printed filament, which keeps the internal structural relationships legible through the material.

Pike Courtyard
Summer '25  ·  Built Work / Professional Collaboration

A courtyard project completed the summer after first year — which makes it unusual before we even get to the work itself. Theo was working with an actual structural engineer, a contractor, and a client during a period when most architecture students are not yet enrolled in their first studio.

The project explored threshold conditions between interior and exterior with integrated landscape elements. Documentation spans perspective renders, plan drawings, SolidWorks modeling, and construction photography of the physical model being built. The professional collaboration context — engineer, client, contractor — meant he was translating design intent across real stakeholders before his second year had started. That is genuinely uncommon.

Triangulated Tectonic Design
Fall '25  ·  Site Response / Civic Program

A town hall for Altadena, designed in the aftermath of the January 2025 Altadena fire. The building emerges from the ground as if formed by the same tectonic forces that shape the surrounding San Gabriel Mountains — the triangulated geometry is derived from the site's terrain and rebuilding context, not from a Grasshopper definition.

This is a different kind of thinking than the computational projects — site-driven, civic-scale, environmentally motivated. Documented through physical model photography and a drawing set.

Forged Fungus
2023  ·  Arkitek IDP Internship  ·  Kharkiv, Ukraine

A pavilion for Specialized School No. 134 in Kharkiv, Ukraine — a school that was overtaken by Russian forces in March 2022. The brief: shelter the students from sun and rain, give them a place to assemble for lunch, and provide access to the bomb shelter below. The design is a mushroom cap that pivots to track shade, with a counterweight that doubles as the bunker door.

Completed during Theo's first architecture internship, in high school, before any studio class. Most of the process was hand drafting; digital work was done in Illustrator on an iPad. Running a real project — from site research through presentation — before formal architectural education is the origin point for the rest of this portfolio.

Axon Voice
2026  ·  Software  ·  Independent Project

A free, fully offline voice-to-text application for Windows — speech becomes text in any application, with no account, no API key, and no audio ever leaving the user's machine. Built in Python with PyQt6 and faster-whisper, designed, coded, and shipped independently.

Axon Voice is representative of how Theo works: he builds tools to remove friction from his own workflow, then finishes them to the point that other people can use them. The same instinct produced this portfolio site — hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no framework, no template.
