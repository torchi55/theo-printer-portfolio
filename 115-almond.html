THEO JANEWAY

115 Almond

Ashland, Oregon · Arkitek · 2026

A new house for an elderly couple who wanted it to be the last one they move into. Their list was specific: a lap pool, a gym, an elevator, a hot tub off the upper deck, and a bedroom for a caretaker who can come and go without walking through the house. The site pushes back — 8,711 sq ft, capped at 45% coverage, falling roughly 15% from Almond Street down to the rear. So the house sits on the slope in two levels. The living floor arrives at street grade; everything heavy, wet or private drops into the hillside below it and opens straight onto the pool terrace. The elevator ties the two together, so the plan still works on the day the stairs stop working.
