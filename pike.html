Program + Goals A1.00

The Pike Courtyard system is built around two constraints: a narrow rectangular yard and two existing palm trees. Every element, from the risers to the tower to the stage, responds to those palms rather than fighting them. The goal was a space that could scale from a 50-person hang to a 1,000-person headline show without structural compromise.

Lineweight Plan · Full Courtyard A1.00
  • 01
    Modular Riser Bay ×2

    Stepped timber risers with 4×4 DF posts and 2×4 framing. Mirrored across the courtyard for symmetry. Each bay supports up to 4,000 lbs. Enough for a packed row at any event.

    Ref A-201
  • 02
    King Tower

    Built-in bar below the tower deck. Two service points, one per courtyard side, aimed to split crowd pressure and prevent the single-bar bottleneck that kills event flow.

    Ref A-301
  • 03
    Performer Platform

    Elevated DJ platform, raised above the crowd and visible from every position in the courtyard. Runs as the primary stage, or as a second DJ stand for back-to-back sets when the main stage needs a changeover.

    Ref A-401
  • 04
    Courtyard Programme

    Circulation. Spread people out of the main dance floor and onto the risers. A capacity of 1,000+ means the space needs clear paths for large-scale events like Diplo.

    Ref A-501

Focal Programme Node

King Tower

Two programme elements stacked vertically, built around the existing palm. Hover the drawing or the list to inspect each callout.

  • 01
    Dual Bar Service

    Bar service below the tower deck, one counter per courtyard side. Splitting the crowd across two service points prevents the single-bar bottleneck. Both bars face outward into the courtyard wings, so traffic flows away from the centre.

  • 02
    Palm Tree

    Retained landscape element. The tower is designed around it, not despite it. The palm becomes part of the tectonic, not an obstacle to it.

  • 03
    Performer Platform

    Elevated DJ platform at the base of the tower. Acts as a second performance deck when the main stage is changing over, or a high-visibility vantage point. Sightlines extend across the full courtyard from this position.

Story A5.00

ΠΚΑ · Pike Courtyard

Designed in Summer 2025 for the Pike chapter at USC. A full-programme riser and stage installation, conceived to give every Pike event the infrastructure it deserves. From Diplo to tailgates to Pike band performances, the system scales.

I designed the full system: risers, King Tower, performer platform, and courtyard circulation, and built it with the chapter. Marcus ran the structural check on the riser framing and load assumptions. The installation has been in continuous use since it went up.
