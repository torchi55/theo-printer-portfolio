Forged
Fungus

Arkitek IDP  ·  2023

Russian forces overtook Kharkiv's Specialized School No. 134 on March 16, 2022. Known for its German-language curriculum, it became a battlefield. City workers collected torched chairs and desks as scrap metal. Children returned, but only to visit the playground — somehow preserved from the fighting.

During my architecture internship I was assigned to design a pavilion for No. 134. Drawing on Lebbeus Woods and Gaudí's organic forms, I conceptualized the Forged Fungus. The vertical carrot acts as a counterweight; the horizontal carrot serves as a blast door to an underground bunker. The mushroom cap pivots throughout the day to shade a ground-level amphitheater, doubling as a stage platform.

This was my first architecture internship — I did it in high school, before I'd taken any studio class. Most of the process was hand drafting; the digital work was Illustrator on iPad. Running a real project from site research through final presentation drawings is what made me decide to study architecture.
