THEO JANEWAY

Forged
Fungus

Arkitek IDP  ·  2023

Russian forces overtook Kharkiv's Specialized School No. 134 on March 16, 2022. Known for its German-language curriculum, it became a battlefield. City workers collected torched chairs and desks as scrap metal. Children returned, but only to visit the playground — somehow preserved from the fighting.

During my internship I was assigned to design a pavilion for No. 134. Drawing on Lebbeus Woods and Gaudí's organic forms, I conceptualized the Forged Fungus — a mushroom cap that pivots to shade a ground-level amphitheater, with a blast-door carrot and underground bunker below.

This was my first architecture internship, done in high school before any studio class. Most of the process was hand drafting; digital work was Illustrator on iPad. Running a real project from site research through final presentation is what made me decide to study architecture.
