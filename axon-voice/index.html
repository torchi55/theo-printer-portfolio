Voice dictation for Windows that runs on your own PC.

Hold Ctrl Space, talk, let go. Axon types what you said into whatever app has the cursor. The speech model runs on your machine, so there is no account, no subscription, and no audio upload.

What it does.

Hold-to-TalkCtrl+SpaceHold, talk, let go. For a sentence or two.
Hands-FreeCtrl+Alt+SpacePress once to start and again to stop. For long dictation.
Paste at the cursorany appEmail, code, chat, docs. It pastes first, and types the text out instead in apps that block paste.
Snippetskeyword → textSay a keyword and Axon types the saved expansion.
Filler cleanupoptionalRemoves “um” and “uh” so you never backspace them.
Vocabularynames, jargonA list of terms it should always spell right.
ModelautoReads your GPU and runs the largest Whisper model it can handle.
Tray appstarts at loginOne window, one tray icon. It stays out of the way until you press the hotkey.

What it doesn't do

  • No AI rewriting of your grammar or tone.
  • No “voice agent” that sends your emails.
  • No 99-language cloud models.
  • No Mac version yet.

Where your data lives

  • Audio is transcribed on your PC and never uploaded.
  • History, snippets and settings: %APPDATA%\AxonVoice
  • No telemetry. The only network request is the one-time model download, 75 MB to 1.5 GB depending on your GPU.

Axon does fewer things than the paid apps, and that is the point. It is built by one person, for friends, family, and anyone who'd rather talk than type.

Compared with the paid apps.

The popular apps are good. Most of them send your voice to the cloud, charge monthly, or keep the useful parts behind a paywall. Axon trades the extras for free, offline, and no account.

Windows will warn you first

Axon isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows flags it as “Unknown publisher.” It's not a virus, and the source is public. Two clicks get past it:

  1. When you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, the small text under the warning.
  2. A Run anyway button appears at the bottom. Click it.
